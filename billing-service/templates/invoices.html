SSO Test - Success! 🎉

Congratulations! You successfully accessed this protected billing dashboard using Single Sign-On (SSO). Notice that you didn't have to log in again after accessing the ProfileService.

This demonstrates how SSO works:

  • You logged in once at the AuthService
  • The AuthService created a session cookie in your browser
  • When you accessed ProfileService, you were redirected to AuthService
  • AuthService saw you were already logged in and issued a JWT
  • Now when accessing BillingService, the same process happened seamlessly

Key Point: Each service maintains its own session, but authentication is centralized. This is the essence of Single Sign-On!
